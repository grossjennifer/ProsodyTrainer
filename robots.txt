# A note on the AI crawlers, since they do two different jobs and you may not
# want to treat them the same way:
#
#   Retrieval / citation crawlers fetch a page to answer a live question and
#   can cite you. These are the ones that matter for being found:
#     OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User,
#     PerplexityBot, Google-Extended (for AI Overviews)
#
#   Training crawlers collect text to train future models. They do not send
#   readers back to you:
#     GPTBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot, Applebot-Extended, meta-externalagent
#
# The Allow: / above permits both. If you ever want citation without
# contributing to training corpora, uncomment the block below. Be aware this
# is a real trade-off and the line between the two categories keeps moving.
