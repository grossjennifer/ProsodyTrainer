The earlier study also identified QID60, QID64, QID56, QID52, and QID51 as
the strongest incongruent items. Those items should not be copied into the new
measure as foils. They were different passages from the congruent set, so their
old markings do not define a matched incorrect version. Their value is as a
source of candidate metrical patterns after each passage is independently
re-annotated.

## Selection rule for the upcoming measure

The strongest legacy passages should not replace the new prose items. They
should contribute a small number of anchor patterns, ideally no more than two
or three of the final items. Each retained passage should be converted into:

1. One unaltered sentence or line.
2. A keyed version with the intended syllables marked.
3. A foil with the same number of marked syllables, placed on comparable
   content words whenever possible.

The foil should not be created by simply moving every mark to function words.
That would recreate the confound identified during development of the new
measure. Items with a genuinely competing conventional rhythm should either
become explicitly labeled ambiguity probes or be replaced.

## Recommended order of work

1. Confirm the syllable-level scansion for QID62, QID54, QID67, QID57, and
   QID66 with the Rhythm Reader and expert review.
2. Draft matched foils while preserving the number of marked syllables and
   the content/function balance.
3. Pilot the legacy-derived items separately from the existing 30-item pool.
   Retain an item only if participants distinguish the key from the foil and
   the item does not become a poem-recognition or function-word strategy.
